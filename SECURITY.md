# Security policy

Do not report exposed credentials, booking references, personal email data, or traveler records in a public issue.

Until a private reporting address is selected, stop and contact the repository owner through a private channel already known to you. Do not create a public proof of concept or retain copies of personal trip data. Maintainers should rotate exposed credentials immediately, inspect history and release artifacts, and follow the remediation procedure in [docs/SECURITY.md](docs/SECURITY.md).

TRIPPY is pre-release software. No version is currently declared supported for security updates.
