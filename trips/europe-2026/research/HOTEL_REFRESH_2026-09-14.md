## Decision rule

Do not select purely on a starting rate. For the finalists, compare the exact same room/cancellation terms across Booking, Vio, Expedia, Priceline, trivago and direct hotel before selecting. Keep a candidate only if its final all-in price is below EUR 300/night and its transit burden does not erase the room-price advantage.
