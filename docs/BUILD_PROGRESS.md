# Build progress against the product roadmap

Source assessment · 2026-10-14 · local candidate, not a deployment receipt.

This ledger accounts for all **62** IDs in [the product roadmap](PRODUCT_ROADMAP.md). It distinguishes implemented bounded behavior from the broader acceptance target. It does not mark the whole roadmap complete. **Current accounting: 15 implemented, 43 partial, 3 planned, 1 external dependency.** Current source may be ahead of the hosted demo; no publication, deployment, live inventory, eligible provider access or remote database validation is established here.

**Implemented** means the named behavior has a concrete supported local path and contract; external operation still needs its own acceptance. **Partial** means useful source exists but part of the row's acceptance target remains. **Planned** means the listed extension does not yet have a supported complete path. **External dependency** means the remaining acceptance requires an authorized operator-owned environment or service, and source alone cannot establish it.

## This implementation wave

- Sample **Cities**, **All activities** and **Hotels & days** add device-only saved filter presets, active chips and clear-all. Generic **Find in trip** opens the exact current-record context without selecting a route/quote or changing a task. Generic/detailed maps show only explicit destination/activity/stay pins and inspect their sources; transport booking endpoints/times remain separately reviewed metadata. Evidence: [`filter-presets.ts`](../src/lib/filter-presets.ts), [`workspace-search.ts`](../src/lib/workspace-search.ts), [`planning-map.ts`](../src/lib/planning-map.ts).
- Generic **Route & map → Compare the whole journey** adds recorded scenario cards, partial budgets, per-component gaps and a conservative equivalent-cost gate. Leg elapsed time and separately recorded booking transfer times never establish a door-to-door total. Hotel-base context uses sourced straight-line pin distances across same-city planning days, with unknowns and receipts; it does not infer routes or travel times. Evidence: [`journey-comparison.ts`](../src/lib/journey-comparison.ts), [`journey-comparison.test.mjs`](../tests/journey-comparison.test.mjs).
- **Plan together → Route & stay ideas** adds pending share-safe ideas and owner review. Accepted ideas are immutable board planning records; they do not create bookings or select private workspace options and remain excluded from reviewed owner-workspace import. Existing proposal discussion/history/access rules apply. Evidence: [`collaboration-planning-ideas.test.mjs`](../tests/collaboration-planning-ideas.test.mjs).

- `/plan` → **Trip tools → Detailed cities & days** mounts `CanonicalTripWorkspace` through the versioned `buildCanonicalAtlas` adapter. City rails, activity grids, day columns and the detail inspector are shared with the atlas. Arbitrary trips retain their own dates, IDs, explicit coordinates, canonical booking states and receipts. Missing geography, images, priorities, stay dates and prices remain unknown. City pins, day assignments and activity records can now be edited through validated workspace callbacks with shared undo; category, priority, sourced coordinates and attributed photos remain optional. Full atlas route/transport/hotel editing parity and migration of the specialized sample into a compatibility pack remain incomplete. Evidence: [`canonical-atlas.ts`](../src/lib/canonical-atlas.ts), [`trip-workspace.tsx`](../src/components/trip-workspace.tsx), [`canonical-atlas.test.mjs`](../tests/canonical-atlas.test.mjs).
- `/plan` → **Saved trips on this device** previews and revalidates up to 40 indexed saves plus the last saved legacy workspace. Explicit save indexes a validated workspace; listing does not mutate storage. Complete-workspace JSON import validates and previews before acceptance into memory. This is not account sync, a cross-surface trip registry or a general schema migration engine. Evidence: [`workspace-library.ts`](../src/lib/workspace-library.ts), [`workspace-library.tsx`](../src/components/workspace-library.tsx), [`new-trip-planner.tsx`](../src/components/new-trip-planner.tsx), [`workspace-library.test.mjs`](../tests/workspace-library.test.mjs).
- `/plan` → **Research → Search** prepares at most 24 exact one-way cash-flight airport/date queries, displays the scope before an explicit run, uses two bounded workers, supports cancellation and rejects stale generations. Successful-empty, found, failed, locked, unconfigured, cancelled and unsearched scope remain distinct. A reviewed receipt can replace one cash coverage-ledger row. Awards, through tickets/open jaws, hotels and other modes remain unsearched; live eligible-provider operation was not established by mock tests. Evidence: [`research-matrix.ts`](../src/lib/research-matrix.ts), [`research-matrix.tsx`](../src/components/research-matrix.tsx), [`research-matrix.test.mjs`](../tests/research-matrix.test.mjs).
- `/collaborate` prioritizes the loaded board/catalogue and puts setup, invitations and recovery in disclosures. Activity/day proposals remain separate from applied edits; only an owner can apply/reject through an observed-revision transaction. Discussion adds one current choice per owner/editor access capability and bounded comments on pending proposals. Capability pseudonyms are **not verified person identities**; shared invitations share a choice. Discussion cannot apply a proposal or edit bookings. Source/history, role, expiry, revocation and conflict constraints are server-enforced. Evidence: [`schema.ts`](../src/lib/collaboration/schema.ts), [`store.ts`](../src/lib/collaboration/store.ts), [`suggestions.ts`](../src/lib/collaboration/suggestions.ts), [`collaboration-interactions.test.mjs`](../tests/collaboration-interactions.test.mjs).
- `/plan` → **Research → Selected budget** counts only explicit selected quote IDs, once for the recorded party/room basis. Cash, points opportunity cost and cash equivalents have separate per-currency partial subtotals and unknown counts; source programs are not merged. Overlap, party, freshness and missing-term warnings remain visible. No FX, automatic alternative selection or complete-trip budget claim is made. Evidence: [`trip-budget.ts`](../src/lib/trip-budget.ts), [`trip-budget.tsx`](../src/components/trip-budget.tsx), [`trip-budget.test.mjs`](../tests/trip-budget.test.mjs).
- Atlas hotels default to compact exact-property rows with a three-stay comparison and contextual details/photos; map and market-rate sections are collapsed by default. Export studio is mounted in atlas and generic workspaces with scope/layout/theme controls, a sandboxed reading preview, measured HTML bytes and candidate-photo accounting. Linked preview images require opt-in. Selected reading dates filter dated days/movements/bookings while overlapping stay dates remain intact. Remote image bytes, final PDF size and image resizing are not promised. Evidence: [`trip-workspace.tsx`](../src/components/trip-workspace.tsx), [`hotel-comparison.ts`](../src/components/hotel-comparison.ts), [`export-studio.tsx`](../src/components/export-studio.tsx), [`export-preview.ts`](../src/lib/export-preview.ts).

- `/plan` → **History** previews new local scenario identities and explicit booking-copy choices; named snapshots provide complete-workspace restore diffs, stale-base checks and safety-copy/discard decisions. Neither path saves to accounts or indexes a device-library plan automatically. Evidence: [`workspace-scenarios.ts`](../src/lib/workspace-scenarios.ts), [`workspace-history.ts`](../src/lib/workspace-history.ts), [`workspace-history-tools.tsx`](../src/components/workspace-history-tools.tsx).
- `/plan` → **Plan together** reviews reverse board edits against an original share-safe base, with explicit field choices and conserved private planning sidecars. `/collaborate` optionally announces short-lived capability presence and receives bounded authenticated revision notices while its four-second guarded polling continues. See [live collaboration](LIVE_COLLABORATION.md) for identity, deletion, scheduling and hosting limits.
- Shared **Display** controls persist liquid-glass/solid surfaces, compact/comfortable density and reduced motion locally. CSS honors device transparency/motion preferences; this is not an independent accessibility acceptance receipt.

## Verification and handoff boundary

This source update adds focused synthetic tests for saved filter privacy/stale writes, namespaced explicit-pin maps, exact-record workspace search, recorded journey comparison and owner-reviewed shared route/stay ideas. Existing coverage includes stay/transport metadata, preparation, export conservation, shared history, scenarios, reviewed group exchange and presence/notices. Focused tests exercised SQLite/store/route behavior, stale previews, aborts, access switches and sidecar conservation; Postgres/provider/hosted acceptance was not performed. Current repository/browser results belong in [the release report](RELEASE_REPORT.md), with commands, scope and actual outcomes supplied by the integration owner. Historical receipt counts must not be treated as current counts.

The next decisions are full detailed route/transport/hotel editing parity, cross-surface trip/migration boundaries, broader sourced multimode research and independent accessibility/operational acceptance. Deployment, real providers, cloud storage, private imports, notifications and booking actions retain their separate authorization requirements.
