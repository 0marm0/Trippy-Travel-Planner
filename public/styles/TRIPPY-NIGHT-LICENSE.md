# TRIPPY Night style notice

`trippy-night.json` is a palette-only adaptation of the OpenFreeMap Bright
style. It retains the upstream data sources, sprite, fonts, layer structure,
and cartographic design. The palette adaptation uses blue-black land and
water, blue-slate parks, graded blue/gray roads, restrained gold primary
roads, and light labels with dark halos.

Source style: https://tiles.openfreemap.org/styles/bright
OpenFreeMap styles: https://github.com/hyperknot/openfreemap-styles
OSM Bright: https://github.com/openmaptiles/osm-bright-gl-style
