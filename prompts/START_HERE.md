# Start here: set up Trippy and plan a complete trip

Copy the following into an agent that can work with your Trippy checkout. It is a workflow for supported capabilities, not permission to book, publish, access private messages or spend beyond an agreed research budget.

> Help me set up Trippy and plan my trip comprehensively. Read `AGENTS.md`, `docs/AGENT_WORKFLOWS.md`, `prompts/SETUP_GUIDE.md`, `prompts/NEW_TRIP_GUIDE.md`, `docs/INTEGRATIONS.md` and `docs/RESEARCH_PLAYBOOK.md` before acting. Use the actual implementation and my installed tools; preserve unknowns and manual fallbacks.
>
> Start with one compact batch of missing questions about travelers, acceptable origin airports/stations, hard dates and flexible windows, trip length, fixed events, must/optional/rejected cities, interests, pace, total budget and currency, cabin, baggage, lodging/rooms, accessibility, cancellation needs, and rail/car/coach/ferry/overnight/connection tolerance. Ask which points programs and transferable balances I want to consider, my valuation, transfer ratios/bonuses/delays and positioning tolerance. Record assumptions and hard constraints; never inherit the sample trip's cities or dates.
>
> Explain manual mode first. Offer user-owned SerpApi and Seats.aero configuration only when useful, and have me enter keys privately in ignored `.env.local` or my host's secret manager. Never request a key in chat or print it. Discover the actual travel tools available in my agent host, including any hotel, flight, activity, map, rail and car tools. Explain each tool's scope, costs, coverage, freshness and fallback. A named provider such as Kayak or Expedia is not proof of an installed connector or direct website adapter. Verify provider/host support before giving installation instructions.
>
> Build at least three materially different routes with city order, night allocations, gateways/open jaws and transport strategy. Then research a disclosed date/airport/program/mode matrix within my budget. Compare cash and award flights, direct and sensible connections, round trips versus open jaws/one-ways, positioning, alternate airports, rail, car and other plausible modes. Broaden the search around promising results and important gaps. Keep a search ledger, evidence timestamps and unsearched combinations. Compare whole-trip cash, points plus taxes/fees, transfer risk, door-to-door time, extra lodging, luggage, cancellation terms and disruption protection. Do not call a route globally cheapest or invent missing award space.
>
> Research lodging across supported APIs, my authorized host tools and official property sources. Compare equivalent dates, occupants, room types, all-in totals, cancellation, amenities and points options. Score hotel location against airports/stations, neighborhood clusters, attractions and the actual day plan; the lowest nightly rate can cost more in transport or lost time.
>
> Build a broad attributable activity catalogue for every candidate/selected city, including neighborhoods, signature sights, museums/history, food/evening, walks, events, day trips and accessible/weather alternatives. Verify coordinates, hours, closures, ticket/reservation requirements, duration, prices and transit assumptions where possible. Keep alternatives and exclusions with reasons. Cluster feasible days around arrival/departure, hotel check-in/out, luggage, opening hours, timed anchors, meals, rest and realistic travel buffers.
>
> Track flights, rail, car, stays and activities using `recommended`, `quoted`, `held`, `confirmed`, `missing` and `conflict`, keeping evidence separate from recommendations. Obtain specific authorization before private import, booking, email/calendar actions, publication or deployment. Keep booking references, traveler contacts, loyalty/seat details, raw email and all credentials outside public/exportable records.
>
> Maintain a validated editable trip record, separate itinerary sidecar and research/recorded quote evidence. Use the accepted generic manual workspace for routes, supplied coordinate maps, days, activities and reviewed booking changes; keep unknowns explicit. Use `/plan` for normalized draft preview/download and the actual supported atlas controls for the sample. Explain that accepted arbitrary drafts open their own manual workspace; they do not replace every specialized sample-atlas view. If we want friends to edit, read `prompts/COLLABORATIVE_TRIP_GUIDE.md`, prepare the generic share-safe activity-board JSON, preview/import it in a supported durable `/collaborate` setup, review the board, and use owner/editor/viewer capabilities. Live rooms synchronize activities and days separately from bookings and the rich atlas. Preserve unsaved drafts on revision conflicts; never share my provider keys or send invitations without authorization.
>
> Finish with a route comparison, date/price search ledger, cash-versus-points calculation, hotel/location comparison, broad city catalogue, feasible editable day plan, booking-gap list and safe sharing options. State which evidence is fresh, historical, estimated, missing or unsearched, which constraints remain unresolved, and the next decision that matters most. Verify the actual implemented flow before calling setup complete.

The detailed guides are authoritative for schema fields, adapter request examples, provider limits, privacy and current UI scope. Research ledgers and private plans stay outside tracked samples; do not add unsupported fields to a strict schema merely to record research notes.

Optional accounts store sanitized manual workspaces only after explicit upload; configure user-owned storage/secrets and follow [the account contract](../docs/PRIVATE_ACCOUNTS.md). Preserve existing visual/compact export options and use [the format matrix](../docs/EXPORTS.md). Research/quote sidecars and room boards remain separate contracts, not automatic account/provider synchronization.
